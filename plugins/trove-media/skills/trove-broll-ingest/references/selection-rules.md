# Selection rules

A select is one moment, not a stretch of footage that happens to contain one.
Read the contact sheet before deciding, and name the moment in one sentence
before cutting anything. If the sentence has no specific second in it, the
moment is not found yet.

## What earns a select

- The frame it opens on is already the action. A shot that begins on an empty
  frame, a glance at the lens, or the moment before the action starts wastes the
  opening and reads as a mistake.
- The action completes inside the select. Cut after the payoff, not during it.
- An action with a clear shape splits into its parts. Arriving, doing the thing,
  and leaving are three selects, not one long one. Each stands alone when an
  edit drops it in.

## What does not

- A moment you cannot point at. "Some nice footage around the middle" is not a
  select.
- Footage where the subject is still entering or leaving and nothing has
  happened yet.
- A second angle of a moment already selected, unless the user asked for
  coverage.

## How many

Most raw clips yield between none and four. Zero is a valid result. Say so and
catalog nothing rather than lowering the bar to produce an entry.
