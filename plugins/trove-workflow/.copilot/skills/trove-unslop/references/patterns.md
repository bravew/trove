# Unslop Patterns

A catalogue of AI writing tells with concrete fixes. Grouped by category. Scan, rewrite, add voice, self-audit.

## Contents
- Content
- Language
- Style
- Communication artifacts
- Filler
- Jargon
- Plain speech

## Content

1. **Manufactured significance.** Don't inflate a routine change into a milestone. State what it does.
2. **"It's not just X, it's Y."** Drop the construction. Say Y.
3. **"X isn't about Y, it's about Z."** Same. State Z directly.
4. **False balance.** Don't add a "however, there are tradeoffs" paragraph unless there's a real tradeoff to name.
5. **Restating the prompt** back to the user before answering. Answer.

## Language

6. **Em dash.** Banned. Split into two sentences, or use a colon before a list. The parentheses substitute is also banned.
7. **Mid-sentence colon as a connector.** Banned. Colon before a list is fine.
8. **Rule of three everywhere** ("fast, simple, and reliable"). Use two, or one, when that's the truth.
9. **"Robust", "seamless", "leverage", "utilize", "delve", "boast".** Replace with plain words (use, has, dig into).
10. **"Furthermore / Moreover / Additionally" chains.** Start the sentence with its content.
11. **Gerund openers** ("Leveraging X, we…"). Lead with the subject.

## Style

12. **Hedging stacks** ("it's worth noting that", "it's important to consider", "generally speaking"). Cut.
13. **Weasel quantifiers** ("various", "numerous", "a range of"). Give the number or the list.
14. **Title Case Headers For Everything.** Sentence case.
15. **Bold-everything.** Bold the one thing that matters, not every noun.
16. **Header-plus-dash one-liners** ("**Verification** — ran the suite"). Write the header as its own sentence: "**Verification.** Ran the suite."
17. **Emoji as decoration.** Only when the user asked for them.

## Communication artifacts

18. **"I hope this helps!" / "Let me know if…"** sign-offs. End on the content.
19. **"Great question!" / "You're absolutely right!"** openers. Skip the validation.
20. **Apologizing reflexively.** Fix the thing; don't perform contrition.
21. **Announcing what you're about to do** at length. Just do it, with a one-line note if needed.

## Filler

22. **"In today's fast-paced world" / "In the world of X".** Delete.
23. **"At the end of the day" / "When all is said and done".** Delete.
24. **"It goes without saying".** Then don't say it.
25. **Summary paragraph that repeats the body.** Cut, unless the body was long enough to warrant a recap.

## Jargon

26. **Corporate abstractions** ("synergies", "stakeholder alignment", "operationalize") in engineering prose. Name the concrete thing.
27. **Vague nouns** ("solutions", "capabilities", "offerings") where a specific noun fits.
28. **"Best practices" as a shield.** Name the actual practice and why.

## Plain speech

29. **Long Latinate verbs** ("facilitate", "demonstrate", "necessitate"). Use help, show, need.
30. **Passive voice hiding the actor** ("mistakes were made"). Name who did what.
31. **Abstract before concrete.** Lead with the concrete claim; let the abstraction follow if needed.
