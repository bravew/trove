# Task: Edit a short intro

The user has `intro.mp4`, a 22 second 16:9 talking-head clip that is already
cut, and says "edit this intro: captions, a couple of overlays where I make a
point, and some music." They have a music file at `~/audio/bed.wav` and no
sound effects. python3 and ffmpeg are installed. The skill manual has not been
read this session.

Probe to confirm the mode, transcribe, author an edit plan where each beat
names the words it lands on and why, lint the plan, and render a preview. Stop
and show the preview. Do not render the final, do not write a raw ffmpeg
command, and do not fetch any logo or stock image.
